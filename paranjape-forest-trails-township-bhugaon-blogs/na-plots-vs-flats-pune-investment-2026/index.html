NA Plots vs Flats in Pune — Better Investment in 2026?

Published: September 15, 2026  |  By: PropSmart Realty Team  |  Reading time: ~6 min

One of the most debated questions among Pune real estate buyers is: should you buy an NA plot or a flat? In 2026, with property prices rising across Pune West and new infrastructure projects reshaping connectivity, this question is more relevant than ever. This guide offers a clear, data-backed comparison to help you decide.

What Is an NA Plot? Quick Primer

An NA (Non-Agricultural) plot is a piece of land that has been officially converted from agricultural use to residential/commercial use under Maharashtra's Land Revenue Code. Key characteristics:

  • Legally cleared for construction of a home or villa
  • Must be RERA-registered (from 2017 onward) if sold by a developer
  • Clear, freehold title — you own the land, not just a floor
  • Can be financed through bank plot loans (75–80% LTV)

Capital Appreciation — Plots vs Flats

Historically across Pune, NA plots in emerging areas have delivered superior capital appreciation compared to apartments, primarily because land is finite. In Bhugaon and similar Pune West growth corridors:

  • NA Plots (Bhugaon): Estimated 25–40% appreciation over 5 years
  • Flats (Bhugaon / Bavdhan): Estimated 15–22% appreciation over 5 years
  • Villas / Twin Bungalows: 20–30% appreciation, combining land and built-up value

Rental Income — Flats Win Here

Unlike plots (which generate no rental income until constructed), flats offer immediate rental yield:

  • 2BHK in Bhugaon: ₹18,000–₹24,000/month rental
  • 3BHK in Bhugaon / Bavdhan: ₹25,000–₹35,000/month
  • Rental yield: 2.5–3.5% annually in Bhugaon/Bavdhan micro-market

Apartments at Paranjape Forest Trails start from ₹89 L* (RERA: P52100079518) — an accessible entry for rental investors.

Tax Considerations

  • Plots: Long-term capital gains tax (20% with indexation) applies after 2 years of holding. No depreciation benefit.
  • Flats: Home loan principal and interest offer Section 80C and 24(b) income tax benefits — a key advantage for salaried investors.
  • Villas: Full home loan tax benefits applicable.

Risks to Consider

Risks of Buying an NA Plot

  • No income until constructed — holding cost is pure capital
  • Construction management adds complexity if you build later
  • Important to verify NA order, 7/12, and RERA registration

Risks of Buying a Flat

  • Depreciation of building over time (not land)
  • Rising maintenance and society charges
  • Limited customisation, especially in completed projects

What About Twin Bungalows and Villas?

For buyers who want the best of both worlds — land ownership plus a ready luxury home — twin bungalows at ₹2.85 Cr* (RERA: P52100048536) and villas at ₹3.89 Cr* (RERA: P52100031560) at Paranjape Forest Trails offer a compelling proposition. You get immediate occupancy, rental potential, and significant land component driving appreciation.

Our Verdict for 2026

If your goal is maximum long-term wealth creation and you can hold for 5+ years, an NA plot in a growing corridor like Bhugaon is hard to beat. If you need rental income or immediate occupancy, a 2BHK or 3BHK apartment in the same township gives you better cash flow. The smartest strategy? Many investors buy both — a plot for long-term appreciation and a flat for rental yield.
