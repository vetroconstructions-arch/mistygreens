Paranjape Athashri Senior Living Review 2026 — Is It Worth It?

Published on September 08, 2026 • By Paranjape Senior Living Research Cell • 8 min read • Senior Housing Assessment

As India's demographics evolve and nuclear family setups become common across metro cities, thousands of working professionals and NRI children find themselves wrestling with a sensitive dilemma: How can we ensure our aging parents enjoy independent, dignified, and medically secure golden years without feeling isolated or institutionalized?

Over 25 years ago, Paranjape Schemes (Construction) Ltd addressed this societal necessity by conceptualizing "Athashri" — India's very first purpose-built senior citizen housing community. Unlike conventional care facilities, Athashri was born from a philosophy of celebrative retirement living, where elders reside among intellectual peers in resort-like comfort.

In 2026, the crowning jewel of this concept is Athashri at Paranjape Forest Trails, Bhugaon (MahaRERA: P52100077686). Nestled within a 190-acre green forest canopy just minutes from Kothrud and Bavdhan, this dedicated enclave offers 2 BHK senior-friendly homes starting at ₹83 Lakhs*. But does Athashri live up to its sterling reputation? In this honest 2026 review, we analyze safety, medical support, dining quality, pricing, and everyday resident happiness.

1. The Athashri Difference: Active Community vs Isolated Old Age Homes

The most important distinction to understand is that Athashri is not an old age home. It is a premium, privately owned, freehold residential community exclusively designed for senior citizens aged 55 and above. Residents hold clear statutory ownership of their flats, enjoying complete financial autonomy and generational inheritance rights.

At Forest Trails Bhugaon, Athashri benefits immensely from its master-planned setting:

  • Uncompromised Nature: Surrounded by over 30,000 indigenous trees, seniors awaken to pure mountain breezes from the Sahyadri foothills, with an Air Quality Index (AQI) routinely below 50. For seniors with respiratory issues, chronic asthma, or cardiac sensitivities, this microclimate is transformative.
  • Intergenerational Warmth: Because Athashri sits inside the broader 190-acre Forest Trails township, residents don't feel sequestered from normal society. Children from nearby enclaves play in surrounding parks, and the Sri Sri Ravishankar Vidya Mandir (SSRVM) school inside the gates infuses life and youthful energy into the environment.

For children living across the globe in North America, Europe, or other Indian metros, healthcare readiness is the primary concern. Athashri Bhugaon has engineered a multi-tiered emergency protocol:

3. Age-Friendly Architectural Details: Designing for Longevity

Falls and domestic slips are among the most serious hazards facing elders. Athashri flats at Forest Trails are purposefully detailed to eliminate architectural friction:

  • Zero Thresholds: Complete barrier-free floor transitions from room to room, allowing seamless wheelchair and walker maneuverability.
  • Anti-Skid Flooring: Specialized slip-resistant vitrified tiles applied in living areas, kitchens, and especially bathrooms.
  • Ergonomic Grab Rails: Sturdy stainless steel grab bars installed along shower areas and wall-hung water closets (EWC).
  • Elevators with Stretcher Capacity: High-speed dual elevators designed specifically to accommodate emergency stretchers and medical beds.
  • Gentle Gradients: All township access pathways, garden trails, and clubhouse entryways feature gentle ramps alongside handrails.

4. Daily Dining, Nutrition & Housekeeping Services

Cooking daily meals and managing domestic help often becomes a stressful burden for seniors. Athashri eliminates this friction through curated on-site hospitality services:

Pure, Nutritious Dining Hall

Athashri features a dedicated dining facility serving 4 wholesome meals daily: morning tea & breakfast, nutritious lunch, evening snacks, and light dinner. Menus are balanced under geriatric nutritionist supervision, catering to low-sodium, diabetic, and jain dietary preferences. Residents have the freedom to eat at the dining hall or have tiffin meals delivered directly to their doorstep.

In addition, Athashri's dedicated facility management handles periodic housekeeping, deep cleaning, plumbing, electrical maintenance, and waste management, ensuring seniors never have to struggle with unreliable external domestic contractors.

5. Social Fabric, Recreation & Intellectual Engagement

Loneliness is the silent epidemic among modern retirees. At Athashri, the social calendar is rich and self-directed:

  • Library & Reading Rooms: Stocked with regional and national dailies, periodicals, spiritual texts, and classic literature.
  • Daily Wellness & Yoga: Gentle yoga, laughter clubs, pranayama classes, and guided meditation every morning in the landscaped amphitheatre.
  • Celebrations & Cultural Programs: Festivals like Diwali, Ganesh Utsav, Makar Sankranti, and Independence Day are celebrated with enthusiasm, musical performances, and drama clubs.
  • Indoor Recreation: Dedicated carrom, chess, bridge, and card rooms where residents congregate daily for friendly intellectual sparring.
