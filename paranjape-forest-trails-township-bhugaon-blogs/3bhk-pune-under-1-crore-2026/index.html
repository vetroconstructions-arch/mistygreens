3BHK in Pune Under 1 Crore — Is It Still Possible in 2026?

Published: September 15, 2026  |  By: PropSmart Realty Team  |  Reading time: ~6 min

"I want a 3BHK in Pune for under ₹1 crore. Is that even possible in 2026?" — This is one of the most common questions we receive at PropSmart Realty. The honest answer: Yes, it's still possible — but only if you know where to look. As Pune's core real estate market has heated up significantly, the ₹1 crore 3BHK is increasingly a product of the city's emerging growth corridors rather than established areas. Let's break it down.

The Brutal Truth About 3BHK Prices in Established Pune Areas

In Pune's well-known residential zones, a 3BHK will cost you significantly more than ₹1 crore in 2026:

  • Kothrud: ₹1.5 Cr – ₹2.5 Cr for a decent 3BHK
  • Bavdhan: ₹1.2 Cr – ₹1.8 Cr
  • Baner / Aundh: ₹1.8 Cr – ₹3 Cr+
  • Wakad / Pimple Nilakh: ₹1.2 Cr – ₹1.7 Cr
  • Viman Nagar / Kalyani Nagar: ₹2 Cr – ₹4 Cr+

If your budget cap is ₹1 crore and you want a 3BHK, you need to look at Pune's emerging micro-markets — and in western Pune, the standout option is Bhugaon.

Where Can You Still Find a 3BHK Under ₹1 Crore in Pune in 2026?

1. Bhugaon — Western Pune's Best Value Bet

Bhugaon, home to the Paranjape Forest Trails township, offers compact 3BHK / spacious 2BHK apartments starting from ₹89 L* (RERA: P52100079518). These are not cramped budget apartments — they come with:

  • Full township amenities (clubhouse, swimming pool, gym, children's park)
  • Forest and mountain-facing views
  • Gated 24/7 security
  • Walking and cycling trails
  • Paranjape Schemes brand assurance

Bhugaon also has excellent price appreciation potential, making it a smart buy for both lifestyle and investment.

2. Wagholi — Eastern Pune's Budget Corridor

Wagholi remains one of eastern Pune's most affordable 3BHK markets, with options from ₹70–₹95 L in mid-tier projects. However, infrastructure quality and builder credibility vary significantly — due diligence is essential.

3. Undri / Pisoli — Southern Pune

Southern Pune offers 3BHKs in the ₹75–₹1.1 Cr range with reasonable connectivity to Hadapsar and Pune ring road. A good option for IT professionals working in MIDC or Hadapsar.

4. Pirangut / Mahalunge Fringe (West)

Beyond Bhugaon, the Pirangut–Mahalunge belt offers budget 3BHK options under ₹90 L, but infrastructure is less mature and developer credibility must be carefully verified.

Why Bhugaon Is the Smart ₹1 Crore 3BHK Choice in 2026

Here's what sets Bhugaon apart from other budget 3BHK markets:

  • ✅ Brand credibility: Paranjape Schemes — 35+ years in Maharashtra, zero project defaults
  • ✅ Township living at apartment price: ₹89 L buys you access to full township infrastructure worth crores
  • ✅ Appreciation potential: Bhugaon is early in its growth curve; Wagholi and Undri have already matured
  • ✅ Flyover impact: Chandani Chowk flyover (Q4 2026) will dramatically improve commute times
  • ✅ Lifestyle quality: Forest, clean air, trails — unmatched among budget 3BHK options

Home Loan Eligibility Guide for ₹1 Crore 3BHK

  • Loan amount needed: ₹72–₹80 L (at 80% LTV on ₹90L–₹1Cr property)
  • Monthly salary needed (approx.): ₹90,000–₹1.1 L/month for standard bank eligibility
  • EMI estimate (20-year, 8.75%): ~₹63,000–₹72,000/month
  • Paranjape projects have pre-approved loan tie-ups with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, and Kotak

Act Now — The Window Won't Last Long

With the Chandani Chowk flyover opening and overall Pune West demand rising, the sub-₹1 crore 3BHK window in quality townships like Paranjape Forest Trails is narrowing. Prices are expected to revise upward once the flyover opens and new inventory is absorbed. If a 3BHK in Pune is on your radar, Bhugaon 2026 is the moment to act.
