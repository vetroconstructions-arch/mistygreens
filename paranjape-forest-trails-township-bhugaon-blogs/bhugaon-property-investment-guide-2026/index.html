Bhugaon Property Investment Guide 2026 — Why Bhugaon Is Pune's Next Hotspot

Published: September 15, 2026  |  By: PropSmart Realty Team  |  Reading time: ~7 min

Tucked at the foot of the Sahyadri mountain range, Bhugaon is rapidly shedding its "outskirts" tag and emerging as one of Pune's most talked-about real estate destinations in 2026. With a major integrated township already underway, improving connectivity, and property prices still below mature western Pune markets, Bhugaon presents a unique window of opportunity. This guide covers everything you need to know before buying property in Bhugaon.

Where Exactly Is Bhugaon?

Bhugaon is a village/gaon located in western Pune (Mulshi taluka), positioned between Chandani Chowk (on the Pune–Mumbai bypass) and the Sahyadri foothills. It sits approximately:

  • 12 km from Chandani Chowk / Kothrud
  • 18–22 km from Hinjewadi IT Park
  • 10 km from Bavdhan
  • 25 km from Shivajinagar / Pune city centre

The area's elevation and forest cover give it a distinct microclimate — cooler, greener, and quieter than central Pune. This is a key lifestyle differentiator driving demand from both end-users and NRI investors.

Why Is Bhugaon Gaining Investor Attention in 2026?

1. Paranjape Forest Trails — A Landmark Township

The single biggest catalyst for Bhugaon's real estate boom is the Paranjape Forest Trails township — a sprawling integrated community offering apartments, twin bungalows, villas, and NA plots across multiple phases. Developed by Paranjape Schemes Construction Ltd (est. 1987), this is one of Maharashtra's largest private township projects in the hills. Sub-projects include:

  • Misty Greens — Apartments & Forest Villas
  • The Canopy — Ultra-luxury Villas
  • Rivolo Residences — Riverside living

2. Infrastructure Push — Chandani Chowk Flyover

The Chandani Chowk grade separator / flyover (near completion in 2026) is Bhugaon's single biggest infrastructure catalyst. It eliminates the notorious Chandani Chowk signal bottleneck, cutting commute times from Bhugaon to Kothrud, Shivajinagar, and Hinjewadi significantly. Property values in direct corridors have already moved 10–15% in anticipation.

3. Pune Ring Road Alignment

The proposed Pune Ring Road passes through or near Bhugaon's western fringes. Once developed, this will unlock massive connectivity to the Mumbai–Pune Expressway, NH48, and Hinjewadi Phase 3 — making Bhugaon a key node in the extended western Pune ecosystem.

4. Nature + Luxury Living Demand

Post-2020, demand for large homes with outdoor spaces and greenery has structurally shifted. Bhugaon — with its forest trails, river views, fresh air, and township amenities — is ideally positioned to meet this demand for decades to come.

Bhugaon: Amenities & Lifestyle

Paranjape Forest Trails is a self-sufficient township with:

  • Clubhouse with swimming pool, gymnasium, and sports courts
  • Walking and cycling trails through natural forest
  • 24/7 gated security with CCTV surveillance
  • Landscaped gardens and children's play areas
  • Nearby schools: Podar International, DY Patil, Symbiosis campuses
  • Hospitals within 10–15 km: Ruby Hall (Hinjewadi), Symbiosis Hospital
  • Retail: Westend Mall, Phoenix Palladium (Wakad) within 20–25 km
