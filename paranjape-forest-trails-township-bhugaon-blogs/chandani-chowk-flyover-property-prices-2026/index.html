Chandani Chowk Flyover Effect on Property Prices 2026 | Bhugaon Bavdhan Analysis

Published: September 15, 2026  |  By: PropSmart Realty Team  |  Reading time: ~6 min

If you've ever driven from Kothrud to Bhugaon or Bavdhan during peak hours, you know the pain of the Chandani Chowk signal — Pune's most notorious traffic choke point. In 2026, that reality is about to change forever with the Chandani Chowk flyover (grade separator) project nearing completion. And where infrastructure moves, real estate follows. This analysis breaks down exactly how this flyover is reshaping property prices along the Bhugaon–Bavdhan corridor.

What Is the Chandani Chowk Flyover?

The Chandani Chowk Grade Separator is an elevated road infrastructure project being executed under PMRDA (Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority). It creates a signal-free crossing at the critical Chandani Chowk junction — the confluence of the Pune–Mumbai Bypass (NH48), Kothrud Road, Bavdhan Road, and routes toward Bhugaon and Mulshi.

Key project details:

  • Type: Grade separated flyover / underpass combination
  • Authority: PMRDA / PWD Maharashtra
  • Status (Sep 2026): Near completion, expected to open Q4 2026
  • Impact radius: Bhugaon, Bavdhan, Sus, Pirangut, Mulshi corridor

The Real Estate Infrastructure Effect — How It Works

Infrastructure development is the single most reliable predictor of residential real estate appreciation in India. The typical impact pattern is:

  1. Announcement Phase: Early-mover investors enter; prices rise 5–10%
  2. Construction Phase: Wider market awareness; prices rise another 10–15%
  3. Completion / Operationalisation: End-users arrive; prices jump 8–15% in 12–18 months
  4. Maturity: Market stabilises at a new higher baseline

The Chandani Chowk corridor is currently in late Phase 2 / early Phase 3, making 2026 potentially the last window before the completion-driven price surge.

Why Bhugaon Benefits More Than Bavdhan

Counterintuitively, Bhugaon — further from the flyover — stands to gain more in percentage appreciation than Bavdhan. Here's why:

  • Lower base price: More room for growth from a ₹7,000–₹9,000/sqft base vs ₹10,000+ in Bavdhan
  • Perceived inaccessibility removed: The flyover dissolves the key mental barrier buyers had about Bhugaon's "distance" — transforming it into a 30-minute commute zone from Kothrud
  • Large township inventory: Paranjape Forest Trails provides the supply pipeline to capture this demand shift
  • Dual upside: Bhugaon benefits from both the flyover AND the proposed Ring Road alignment

Historical Precedent — What Happened After Other Pune Flyovers?

Pune has a clear track record of property appreciation post-major road infrastructure:

  • Katraj–Dehu Road Bypass (2018): Undri and Pisoli saw 18–25% price jump within 2 years of opening
  • Wakad–Hinjewadi junction improvement (2019–20): Residential rates along Wakad Main Road rose 15% in 18 months
  • Baner Road widening (2017): Baner–Balewadi property values appreciated 20%+ within 3 years

The Chandani Chowk flyover is comparable in strategic importance to all three of these.

What Should Buyers Do Now?

Our recommendation: Act before the flyover opens. Once the signal-free road is operational and commute times visibly improve, prices will adjust upward — quickly and permanently. Those who bought in Bhugaon in 2024–2025 have already seen 10–14% appreciation. The next wave is coming.
