Water Supply Infrastructure & Fact Check

1. Captive Water Treatment Plant (WTP)

An industrial-grade filtration and chlorination facility processes incoming water to ensure potable quality standards across every residential kitchen tap.

2. Overhead & Underground Reservoirs

Multi-lakh-litre master storage tanks guarantee uninterrupted pressurized water supply even during regional municipal grid shutdowns.

3. Rainwater Harvesting & Ground Recharge

Integrated contour trenching and recharge pits collect monsoon runoff across 190 acres, continuously replenishing township subsoil aquifers.

4. In-House Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)

100% of residential wastewater is treated through biological STP units and recycled for irrigation of extensive botanical gardens and forest paths.

Power Backup & Energy Reliability

Bhugaon falls under the MSEDCL urban fringe distribution grid, which boasts higher stability than rural feeders. To eliminate blackout disruptions:

  • 100% Common Area Backup: Auto-switch diesel generator (DG) banks power all elevators, staircase lighting, external streetlights, water pumps, and security hubs within 8 seconds of grid failure.
  • In-Home Power Provision: Dedicated points inside apartments and villas allow inverter connectivity for fans, lighting, Wi-Fi routers, and charging points.
  • Solar Common Lighting: Over 35% of spine road and perimeter lighting operates on solar energy, directly lowering monthly society maintenance expenditure.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What are the monthly maintenance charges at Paranjape Forest Trails Bhugaon?

For residential apartments (The Canopy, The Highgardens, Everglades), maintenance charges range from ₹2.50 to ₹3.50 per sq.ft. per month. For NA plot owners at Misty Greens, common infrastructure upkeep is charged annually. Senior living residences at Athashri (from ₹83 L*) and Swaniketan (from ₹79 L*) have specialized service maintenance covering medical aid and dining facilities.

How is water supplied to Paranjape Forest Trails township?

The 190-acre township features a dedicated multi-stage Water Treatment Plant (WTP) with large-capacity underground and overhead storage reservoirs. Water is sourced through licensed municipal/irrigation supplies, extensive ground-water recharging, and integrated rainwater harvesting, ensuring 24x7 pressurized drinking and domestic water.

Is there a PMC water pipeline connection to Bhugaon?

Yes, Bhugaon's rapid integration into the PMRDA and Pune Municipal Corporation infrastructure corridor includes the ongoing regional closed-pipeline water distribution network from Khadakwasla / Temghar reservoirs, drastically eliminating private water tanker dependency.

What services are covered under the township maintenance fee?

The monthly fee covers 24/7 security with RFID boom barriers, CCTV surveillance, daily garbage collection and STP operation, landscape maintenance across 190 acres, common electricity for street lights and elevators, DG power backup maintenance, and upkeep of internal asphalted spine roads.

Is 100% DG power backup provided for flats and villas?

Yes, 100% generator backup is provided for all essential common utilities including high-speed elevators, water pumps, spine road illumination, and security systems, along with specified power point backup inside individual residences.
