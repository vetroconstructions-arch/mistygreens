01. THE NA PLOT LEGAL CHECKLIST +

Key documents to verify: Sanction Letter, RERA Registration, and Nil Encumbrance Certificate.

02. BAVDHAN-BHUGAON CONNECTIVITY 2026 +

The upcoming PMRDA Ring Road and the further upgrade of Paud Road will catalyze Bhugaon as the primary gateway to Mulshi and Konkan. Investment in Forest Trails currently leverages the 'Early Mover' price advantage before the 2026 connectivity peak.

03. FSI & CONSTRUCTION GUIDELINES +

Building your bespoke villa requires adherence to PMRDA construction norms. Forest Trails provides detailed design guidelines that balance your personal architectural heritage with the township's aesthetic harmony. Typically, 1.0 to 1.5 FSI is applicable depending on the plot zone.

04. TAX BENEFITS FOR LAND PLOTS +

Investing in NA plots offers unique wealth preservation advantages. While land itself doesn't attract depreciation, construction loans for secondary residences at Forest Trails offer significant tax deductions under Section 24 and 80C of the Income Tax Act.

05. NRI REPATRIATION & FEMA LAWS +

As per FEMA guidelines, NRIs and OCIs can purchase residential and commercial property in India (excluding agricultural land). Since Misty Greens offers NA bungalow plots, they are fully eligible for NRI purchase. Repatriation of sales proceeds is permitted for up to two residential properties.

06. REMOTE ONSIGHT TOURS & BOOKING +

For international investors, we offer comprehensive Zoom-guided drone tours and live walkthroughs. Digital signatures and remote registration support are available through our dedicated NRI Sales Desk to ensure a seamless cross-border acquisition.

07. PARANJAPE SCHEMES EQUESTRIAN ACADEMY +
