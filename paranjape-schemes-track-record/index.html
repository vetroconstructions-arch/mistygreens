Five Decades of Innovation: The Paranjape Milestones Timeline

1974 — Foundation & Vision

Brothers Shrikant Paranjape and Shashank Paranjape establish the organization with a singular vision: to deliver superior structural engineering and uncompromising legal transparency to Pune's middle and upper-middle class families.

1980s — Transforming Kothrud & Deccan

Pioneers systematic residential community living in Pune West. Paranjape Schemes completes dozens of boutique residential buildings in Kothrud, Karve Road, Model Colony, and Deccan Gymkhana, establishing an impeccable reputation for on-time possession.

1998 — Birth of "Athashri" Senior Housing

Paranjape Schemes identifies a crucial emerging demographic necessity and launches Athashri in Pashan — India's very first purpose-built, dedicated senior citizens' housing community. The concept wins national and international acclaim, blossoming into a nationwide movement.

2006 — Blue Ridge Hinjewadi (138 Acres)

As Pune emerges as India's premier IT hub, Paranjape Schemes launches Blue Ridge in Hinjewadi Phase 1. It integrates 25 high-rise residential towers, an international SEZ IT park, Pune's first riverfront golf driving range, and the Blue Ridge Public School.

2010 — Paranjape Forest Trails Bhugaon (190 Acres)

Unveils the largest private eco-luxury township in Pune West. Forest Trails preserves 30,000+ indigenous trees and introduces world-class equestrian horse riding facilities, The Cliff Club, and multi-generational housing enclaves.

2017–2020 — MahaRERA Era & Swift Adaptation

When the Real Estate Regulatory Act is enacted, Paranjape Schemes emerges as an industry poster child, registering all ongoing and new project phases with 100% statutory escrow compliance and zero project abandonment.

2021–2026 — Plotted Land & Next-Gen Luxury

Responding to surging post-pandemic demand for land equity and open spaces, launches Misty Greens (PMRDA NA bungalow plots starting ₹1.23 Cr*), The Rivolo (bespoke forest villas starting ₹3.89 Cr*), The Cove (duet villas starting ₹2.85 Cr*), and The Canopy (hillside apartments starting ₹89L*).

MahaRERA Certifications & Statutory Escrow Discipline

Paranjape Schemes operates with absolute fidelity to the Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority. All buyer funds are managed through dedicated project-specific scheduled bank escrow accounts pursuant to Section 4(2)(l)(D) of the RERA Act, guaranteeing that funds deposited for a specific phase cannot be diverted.
