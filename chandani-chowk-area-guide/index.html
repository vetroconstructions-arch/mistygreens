What is Chandani Chowk?

The Strategic Nerve Centre of Pune West

Chandani Chowk is Pune's most critical western road junction, located at the intersection of the Pune–Mumbai Expressway service road (NH48), Pashan–Sus Road, Paud Road, Baner Road, and Bavdhan link road. This five-way junction has historically been the gateway to West Pune's rapidly growing residential corridors.

Situated at approximately 18.5350°N, 73.8000°E, Chandani Chowk sits at the confluence of several of Pune's most in-demand micro-markets: Baner, Pashan, Sus, Bavdhan, Warje, and further out along Paud Road — Bhugaon and Pirangut. For commuters, investors, and homebuyers, understanding Chandani Chowk's geography is fundamental to understanding West Pune real estate dynamics.

Once notorious for its massive traffic snarls and multi-kilometre queues, the junction has undergone a dramatic transformation since the completion of the elevated flyover in 2022-23. It is also poised for another leap forward with the Pune Metro Line 3 station — firmly cementing Chandani Chowk as the anchor node of West Pune's infrastructure story.

Its central position in West Pune makes it the default orientation point for residents, delivery services, and navigation apps throughout the corridor.

The Chandani Chowk Flyover

West Pune's Infrastructure Game-Changer — Operational Since 2022-23

The Chandani Chowk elevated flyover, completed and opened in 2022-23, is widely regarded as one of the most impactful urban infrastructure projects in Pune's recent history. Developed by PMC and MSRDC in coordination, the flyover spans the five-armed junction and carries vehicles signal-free over the entire intersection.

Before the flyover, peak-hour commuters spent 30-45 minutes merely crossing Chandani Chowk. Queues stretched 2-3 km along Paud Road and Baner Road. Residents of Bhugaon, Pirangut, and Bavdhan bore the worst impact, effectively being cut off from the city during morning rush hours.

After the flyover, the crossing time has dropped to under 3 minutes. Commuters from Paud Road can now seamlessly merge onto Baner Road for Aundh and Shivaji Nagar, or onto Sus Road for Hinjewadi — without a single traffic signal halt at the junction.

🚗 Flyover Impact at a Glance

  • Junction crossing time: 45 min → under 3 min
  • Bhugaon to Baner: 55-65 min → 20-25 min
  • Bhugaon to Hinjewadi: 75 min → 35-40 min
  • Peak-hour queue: 2-3 km → near zero at flyover
  • Overall commute reduction: 30-40%

Complete flyover network operational. Paud Road–Bhugaon connectivity transformed overnight.

20-25% price appreciation recorded across Bavdhan, Sus, and Bhugaon. New project launches proliferate.

Chandani Chowk Metro Station

Pune Metro Line 3 — Hinjewadi to Shivaji Nagar | Expected 2027-28

🚇
Metro Line 3 Update (2026):

Maha-Metro's Line 3 (Hinjewadi IT Park ↔ Civil Court) is under active construction. The Chandani Chowk station is a key elevated station on this 23.3 km corridor. Civil works in the Chandani Chowk–Bavdhan stretch are progressing. Expected commissioning: 2027-28.

🗺

Line 3 Route

Hinjewadi IT Park Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3 → Maan → Sus Road → Chandani Chowk → Bavdhan → Warje → Vanaz → Ramwadi → Civil Court (Shivaji Nagar). Total: 23.3 km, 23 stations.

⏱

Travel Times (Post-Metro)

Chandani Chowk → Hinjewadi: ~30 min | Chandani Chowk → Civil Court: ~35 min | Full Line 3 journey: ~55 min. Frequency expected every 5-7 minutes at peak hours.

🏗

PPP Model Construction

Line 3 is India's first metro in PPP (Public-Private Partnership) mode, with Tata Realty & Infrastructure as a key partner. This model has accelerated funding and execution compared to traditional public metro projects.

📈

Metro Premium Expected

Properties within 500m-1 km of metro stations historically command a 10-15% premium post-opening. Areas like Bavdhan and Sus (feeding Chandani Chowk station) are expected to benefit most.

🔗

Network Integration

Chandani Chowk Metro will eventually connect to Line 1 (PCMC–Swargate) and Line 2 (Vanaz–Ramwadi) via interchange stations, giving West Pune residents access to the entire city's metro grid.

🌿

Impact on Bhugaon

Forest Trails residents at Bhugaon (5-6 km from Chandani Chowk) will have direct auto/cab access to the metro station, enabling a car-free commute to Hinjewadi IT Park — a first for this pocket of Pune West.

Real Estate Impact of Chandani Chowk Infrastructure

How the Flyover and Metro Are Reshaping Property Values

Infrastructure investment is the single most reliable predictor of real estate appreciation in India's tier-1 cities. Chandani Chowk is a textbook case: the mere announcement of the flyover in 2019 triggered an immediate 8-10% price jump in nearby localities, while actual completion pushed values up a further 12-15% within 18 months.

Within the 5 km Chandani Chowk influence zone, total appreciation from 2019 to 2026 has been 20-25% cumulatively, outpacing Pune's city-wide average of 15% over the same period. This overperformance is driven by three compounding factors:

  • Flyover effect: Direct connectivity premium — 8-12% already captured
  • Metro anticipation: Pre-announcement run-up — 5-8% still unfolding
  • Scarcity premium: Limited land near Chandani Chowk as infill fills up
  • IT corridor proximity: Hinjewadi demand spillover into Sus and Bavdhan
  • Lifestyle demand: Post-pandemic preference for green, less dense zones

🔮 Forward Outlook (2026-2030)

With Metro Line 3 scheduled to open by 2027-28, analysts project an additional 12-18% appreciation in micro-markets within 2-5 km of the Chandani Chowk station. Bhugaon and Bavdhan, currently in the "pre-metro discount zone," are expected to benefit most from the post-opening re-rating.

Commercial Developments Near Chandani Chowk

Retail, Office, and Mixed-Use Projects Driving Footfall

The improved connectivity at Chandani Chowk has catalysed significant commercial investment in the surrounding 5 km zone. Retail developers and office space operators have been quick to capitalise on the area's improved accessibility and growing residential population.

🛒

Xion Mall, Wakad (via Sus Road, ~10 km)

One of West Pune's premium malls, easily accessible from Chandani Chowk via Sus Road. Hosts major retail brands, multiplex, F&B — the go-to lifestyle destination for Sus, Bavdhan, and Bhugaon residents.

🏢

Baner IT & Office Corridor (2 km East)

Baner hosts numerous Grade A office buildings including Rainbow Business Park, EON IT Park, and several standalone commercial complexes. All within 10-15 minutes of Chandani Chowk, making it the primary employment hub for the junction's catchment.

🏪

Bavdhan High Street & Markets (2 km)

A rapidly developing high-street retail zone along Paud Road in Bavdhan, featuring supermarkets, pharmacies, restaurants, gyms, and co-working spaces. Serves as the primary daily needs hub for the Chandani Chowk catchment including Bhugaon.

🏥

Medicover Hospital & Healthcare (Bavdhan, ~3 km)

Major multi-speciality hospitals in the Bavdhan-Chandani Chowk corridor, including Medicover and Sahyadri Hospitals, serve the entire West Pune catchment. Within 15-20 minutes for Forest Trails residents.

📚

Educational Institutions (Pashan / Bavdhan, 2-5 km)

The zone houses reputed schools and colleges including Orchid International School, Heritage School, and is proximate to IISER, NCL, and Pune University — making it attractive for families prioritising education infrastructure.

⛽

Emerging Mixed-Use Corridor Along Paud Road

The Chandani Chowk–Bhugaon stretch of Paud Road has seen new retail, fuel stations, and daily convenience stores emerge rapidly post-flyover. This new commercial strip is reducing residents' dependence on distant urban centres for routine needs.

Why Chandani Chowk Matters for Forest Trails Buyers

The Gateway Junction That Makes Bhugaon Investable

For any prospective buyer or investor evaluating Paranjape Forest Trails at Bhugaon, Chandani Chowk is the single most important external infrastructure element to understand. Located just 5-6 km away via Paud Road, the junction effectively determines the "connectivity score" of the entire Bhugaon corridor.

Prior to the flyover, the 5-6 km distance from Bhugaon to Baner could take over an hour during peak hours. Post-flyover, the same journey takes 20-25 minutes — a transformation that has fundamentally changed Bhugaon's value proposition from a "too remote" fringe location to a "connected green address".

The upcoming Metro Line 3 adds a further dimension: when operational, Forest Trails residents will be able to reach the Chandani Chowk metro station in 10-12 minutes by auto/cab, and from there travel to Hinjewadi in 30 minutes without touching their cars. This seamless, car-free connectivity to the IT hub will likely trigger the next leg of appreciation in Bhugaon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Chandani Chowk — Real Estate & Connectivity Q&A

How does the Chandani Chowk flyover help Forest Trails residents? ▼
The Chandani Chowk flyover provides a direct, signal-free connection between Paud Road/Bhugaon and Baner/Pashan areas. Forest Trails residents can now reach Baner in 20-25 minutes and Hinjewadi IT Park via Sus Road in 35-40 minutes. The flyover has reduced commute times by 30-40% compared to the pre-flyover era, transforming the daily commuting experience for the entire Bhugaon corridor.
When will the Chandani Chowk Metro Station open? ▼
The Chandani Chowk metro station is part of the Pune Metro Line 3 project (Hinjewadi to Shivaji Nagar), being constructed by Maha-Metro in PPP mode. Expected completion is 2027-2028. Once operational, it will directly connect Chandani Chowk to Hinjewadi IT Park (30 min), Shivaji Nagar, and eventually the entire Pune metro network — making it a true game-changer for West Pune commuters.
Is Chandani Chowk a good area to buy property? ▼
Chandani Chowk is a commercial junction, not a residential area per se. However, residential properties within 2-5 km (Bavdhan, Sus, Bhugaon) have benefited enormously from the flyover and upcoming metro. Property prices in this radius have appreciated 20-25% since the flyover's completion. The best investment strategy is to look at emerging residential pockets like Bhugaon that sit within the Chandani Chowk influence zone but still offer green, township-format living at relatively accessible price points.
How far is Paranjape Forest Trails from Chandani Chowk? ▼
Paranjape Forest Trails (Bhugaon) is approximately 5-6 km from Chandani Chowk junction via Paud Road. The drive takes 10-15 minutes in normal traffic, and 20-30 minutes during peak hours. Chandani Chowk is the primary navigation landmark for reaching Forest Trails — virtually all GPS and map apps route visitors through this junction. When the metro opens, residents will be about 12 minutes by auto/cab from the Chandani Chowk metro station.
