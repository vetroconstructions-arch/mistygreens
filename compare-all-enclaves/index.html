❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about choosing between Forest Trails enclaves.

Which enclave at Forest Trails has the best ROI potential?

Misty Greens NA plots have shown the highest capital appreciation (18–22% CAGR since 2021) due to limited supply of NA plots and strong demand. For rental yield, The Canopy and The Highgardens apartments offer 3–3.5% gross yield. Combined total return (appreciation + yield) is highest for Misty Greens plots in the 5-year horizon.

Which Forest Trails enclave is ready to move in?

The Highgardens, Orchard Residences, The Verandah, Misty Greens (plots), The Cove (bungalows), Athashri, and Swaniketan are all currently ready or available for possession. The Canopy and The Rivolo have upcoming possession dates (2027 and Q4 2026 respectively).

Which enclave is best for NRI investors?

For NRIs, Misty Greens (NA plots) offers the highest capital appreciation with minimal management overhead (no tenant hassles). The Canopy 2-BHK apartments are ideal for NRIs wanting rental income + appreciation. The Rivolo villas are a premium HNI NRI investment. All Forest Trails enclaves are RERA registered and FEMA-compliant for NRI purchase.

Which enclave is designed for senior citizens?

Athashri is specifically designed for active senior citizens (60+) with dedicated senior amenities, emergency call systems, and age-friendly design. Swaniketan offers assisted living for seniors needing daily care support. Both enclaves are located within the secure Forest Trails township with access to all township amenities.

Are all 10 Forest Trails enclaves RERA registered?

Yes, all 10 enclaves at Paranjape Forest Trails are registered under MahaRERA. The RERA numbers are: Misty Greens: P52100053834 · The Rivolo: P52100031560 · The Cove: P52100048536 · The Canopy: P52100079518 · The Highgardens: P52100053310 · Athashri: P52100077686 · The Verandah: P52100002194 · Orchard Residences: P52100055710 · Swaniketan: P52100052124 · Everglades: P52100055711.
