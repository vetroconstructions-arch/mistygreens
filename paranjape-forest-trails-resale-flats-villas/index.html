Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are resale properties available at Paranjape Forest Trails Bhugaon?

Yes, verified resale units are available across multiple enclaves including 1 & 2 BHK PRO flats at Everglades (from ₹48.50 L*), 2 & 3 BHK apartments at The Canopy and The Highgardens, senior living residences at Athashri (from ₹83 L*) and Swaniketan (from ₹79 L*), NA plots at Misty Greens (from ₹1.23 Cr*), and premium forest villas at The Rivolo (from ₹3.89 Cr*).

What is the process for buying a resale flat or villa in Forest Trails?

The resale process involves: 1) Title and legal verification with the developer/society, 2) Obtaining an official No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Paranjape Schemes and the registered society, 3) Bank loan NOC / foreclosure letter if funded, 4) Execution and registration of the Sale Deed with applicable Maharashtra Stamp Duty & Registration charges, and 5) Society membership transfer.

Can I get a home loan for a resale property in Forest Trails Bhugaon?

Yes, all major public and private banks including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank provide home loans for resale units at Forest Trails, subject to standard title scrutiny and valuation.

What are the society transfer charges for resale properties?

Under Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act guidelines, standard society transfer fees apply alongside developer NOC clearance. The official resale desk assists buyers and sellers with end-to-end documentation transparency.

How does Forest Trails resale capital appreciation compare to other Pune West locations?

Forest Trails has demonstrated an 18% to 22% CAGR since 2019, consistently outperforming standalone developments in Bavdhan and Kothrud due to its 190-acre master-planned forest ecosystem, operational Cliff Club, and SSRVM ICSE school.
