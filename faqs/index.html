Q1  What is Paranjape Forest Trails?

Paranjape Forest Trails is a 190-acre integrated, forest-themed township located in Bhugaon, West Pune, developed by Paranjape Schemes (Construction) Ltd — one of India's most trusted real estate developers since 1987. The township offers a unique living experience surrounded by the Sahyadri foothills, with lush greenery, forest trails, and world-class amenities.

Q2  Where exactly is Paranjape Forest Trails located?

The township is located at Bhugaon, Paud Road, Pune West, Maharashtra 412115. Key distances:

  • ~5 km from Chandani Chowk

The nearest major junction is Chandani Chowk flyover. GPS: 18.5050°N, 73.7406°E.

Q24  What are my rights as a buyer under RERA?

Under RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority), buyers have the following key rights:

  • Right to accurate information — developer must disclose all project details
  • Timely possession — per the RERA-committed possession date
  • Delay compensation — at SBI MCLR+2% per annum for delays
  • Quality assurance — 5-year structural defect liability on developer
  • RERA Escrow protection — 70% of collections ring-fenced for construction
  • Right to complain — file complaints with MahaRERA if rights are violated
Q25  How do I file a complaint with MahaRERA?

To file a complaint with MahaRERA:

  • Visit maharera.mahaonline.gov.in
  • Register as a complainant under 'Citizen Login'
  • Click 'Complaint Registration' and fill in the form with the project's RERA number
  • Submit with supporting documents (sale agreement, payment receipts, correspondence)
  • Pay the nominal filing fee online
  • MahaRERA adjudicates within 60 days typically

Complaints can be filed for delays, quality issues, or false representations.

Q26  What does RERA escrow mean for my investment?

RERA mandates that developers deposit 70% of all collections (sale proceeds + loans) into a dedicated escrow account that can only be used for construction of that specific project. This protects buyers from fund diversion — a common issue in pre-RERA projects.

Withdrawal from the escrow requires certification by an engineer, architect, and Chartered Accountant. This gives Forest Trails buyers significant financial protection for their investment.

Q27  What is the RERA penalty clause for possession delays?

If a developer fails to deliver possession by the RERA-committed date:

  • Developer must pay interest at SBI MCLR+2% per annum on the amount paid for the delay period
  • Currently this equals approximately 10.75%–11% annual interest
  • Buyers can exit the project and get a full refund with interest if delay exceeds RERA timelines
  • Buyers can also file a complaint with MahaRERA for faster resolution

Financing & Loans

Q28  What is the home loan eligibility for a Forest Trails property?

Home loan eligibility depends on your income, CIBIL score (750+ recommended), existing liabilities, and property value:

  • Apartments: Up to 80–85% LTV
  • Plots (NA): Up to 70–75% LTV

For a ₹1.23 Cr Misty Greens plot, you may be eligible for a loan up to ₹86L. Forest Trails has active tie-ups with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, and Kotak for pre-approved financing.

Q29  What is the difference between a home loan and a plot loan?

Key differences between home loan and plot loan:

  • Interest rate: Plot loans typically 0.25–0.5% higher than home loans
  • LTV (Loan-to-Value): Plots: 70% | Apartments: 80–85%
  • Usage: Plot loan is only for land purchase — a separate construction loan is needed to build
  • GST: Not applicable on plot purchases (applicable on under-construction flats)
  • Tax benefit: Plot loan principal repayment qualifies under Sec 80C; interest qualifies only after construction

Q33  How is stamp duty calculated for a Forest Trails property purchase?

In Maharashtra (PMRDA area — Bhugaon falls under PMRDA jurisdiction):

  • Stamp duty: 5% for male buyer | 4% for female buyer (1% concession)
  • Registration charges: 1% of property value (max ₹30,000 for residential)

Example — Misty Greens plot at ₹1.23 Cr (male buyer):

  • Stamp duty = ₹6.15 Lakhs (5%)
  • Registration = ₹30,000 (capped)

Q37  Can NRIs repatriate sale proceeds from Forest Trails properties?

Yes, NRIs can repatriate sale proceeds of up to 2 residential properties. Key conditions:

  • Property must have been purchased through NRE/NRO account
  • Repatriation of principal limited to the original purchase amount in foreign exchange
  • Capital gains repatriated from NRO account subject to TDS deductions
  • RBI annual limit: USD 1 million per year
  • A CA certificate (Form 15CA/15CB) is required

Q39  What capital gains tax applies on sale of Forest Trails property?

As per the Finance Act 2024:

  • LTCG (held 24+ months): 12.5% without indexation OR 20% with indexation — whichever is more beneficial
  • STCG (held under 24 months): Added to income; taxed as per your slab
  • NRI-specific: TDS at 20% (LTCG) or applicable slab rate (STCG) deducted at source by the buyer

Consult your CA or tax advisor for personalised advice.
