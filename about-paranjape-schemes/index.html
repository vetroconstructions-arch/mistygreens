A Tradition of Excellence.

Founded in 1987 by the visionary brothers Shashank and Srikant Paranjape, Paranjape Schemes (Construction) Ltd. has evolved from a local developer into a multi-city powerhouse. With over 200 successfully delivered projects, we have touched the lives of over 40,000 families.

Our philosophy is simple: we don't just build structures; we build communities. Whether it's the pioneering 'Athashri' senior living concept or the massive 190-acre Forest Trails township, our projects are designed with a deep understanding of human needs and ecological balance.

The Vision Behind Forest Trails

Forest Trails Bhugaon is the crown jewel of our West Pune portfolio. A 190-acre gated township that harmonizes luxury living with the Sahyadri Sahyadri mountain range. Our goal was to create a sanctuary where you don't just 'live' but thrive amidst nature.
