Bhugaon Real Estate Historical Price Appreciation (2019 – 2026)

Bhugaon has transformed from a quiet peri-urban scenic corridor into West Pune's premier hillside residential destination. Driven by multi-crore infrastructure projects including the Chandani Chowk multi-level flyover, the PMRDA 128m Ring Road package, and Paud Road commercial expansion, land and residential values in Bhugaon have appreciated at an annualized CAGR of 12.8% to 15.4% over the past seven years (past performance is not indicative of future returns).

Rental Yields & Capital Value Growth Drivers in West Pune

Beyond capital appreciation, residential units in Bhugaon command robust rental yields ranging between 3.6% and 4.2% annually, bolstered by high demand from Hinjewadi IT professionals, Kothrud professionals seeking greener living environments, and families enrolled in the on-campus Sri Sri Ravishankar Vidya Mandir (SSRVM) ICSE school. With continuous infrastructure enhancements including the proposed Paud Road Metro connectivity and the PMRDA 128m Ring Road interchange, Bhugaon represents one of Pune's most resilient micro-markets.
