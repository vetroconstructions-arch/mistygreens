The absolute pinnacle of branded land ownership within Paranjape Forest Trails.

If you are exploring NA plots in Bhugaon but desire a canvas that allows for uncompromising architectural ambition, Kaleidoscope is your final destination.

The Art of the Layout

Standard plotting schemes, even premium ones, often prioritize maximizing the number of salable units. Kaleidoscope NA Bungalow Plots actively reject this philosophy. The master plan for this specific cluster was engineered around elevation, privacy, and expansive sightlines.

The layout ensures that each parcel commands its own distinct view of the 190-acre township and the sprawling Sahyadri ranges. The internal road network winding through Kaleidoscope is wider, the landscaping is more bespoke, and the spacing between plots ensures acoustic and visual privacy for your future multi-level estate.
