Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the overall resident rating for Paranjape Forest Trails Bhugaon?

Paranjape Forest Trails holds an aggregate rating of 4.9 out of 5 based on 1,247 verified homeowner reviews, with top marks for air quality, gated security, active sports amenities, and community living.

Is commuting to Hinjewadi IT Park and Kothrud convenient from Forest Trails?

Yes, with the completion of the multi-level Chandani Chowk interchange, residents reach Kothrud in 10-12 minutes and Hinjewadi IT Park Phase 1-3 in approximately 20-25 minutes via the Mumbai-Bengaluru Highway bypass.

How is the air quality inside the township compared to central Pune?

Due to its 190-acre elevation and 90+ acres of dense tree canopy, the Air Quality Index (AQI) at Forest Trails consistently measures between 35 and 65 (Good / Satisfactory), representing a 60% reduction in PM2.5 pollutants compared to central Pune.

Is Sri Sri Ravishankar Vidya Mandir (SSRVM) located inside the campus?

Yes, SSRVM is an operational ICSE school located directly within the township perimeter, allowing children to walk or cycle to class without venturing onto main traffic highways.

Are senior citizens comfortable living at Athashri Bhugaon?

Athashri Bhugaon (from ₹83 L*) and Swaniketan (from ₹79 L*) are purpose-built senior and assisted living communities with barrier-free architecture, panic alert cords, 24/7 on-call nurses, dining services, and active cultural clubs.
