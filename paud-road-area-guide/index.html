What is Paud Road?

Pune's primary western artery — driving a decade of real estate growth

Paud Road is the primary arterial road running westward from Kothrud's Chandani Chowk junction through Bavdhan, Bhugaon, Pirangut, and all the way to Paud town and deep into the serene Mulshi taluka. Spanning approximately 25 kilometres, this corridor is the backbone of West Pune real estate development.

Over the last decade, Paud Road has transformed from a quiet semi-rural route into one of Pune's most active real estate corridors, driven by IT spillover from Hinjewadi, infrastructure upgrades like the Chandani Chowk flyover, and the region's natural beauty attracting premium lifestyle developments.

The corridor's appeal lies in its price gradient — expensive near Chandani Chowk, transitioning to high-growth mid-range at Bavdhan, and reaching genuine value with strong appreciation potential at Bhugaon and beyond. For investors seeking the ideal entry point, Bhugaon at 8 km represents the sweet spot in 2026.

Each micro-market has its unique character and investment proposition

Chandani Chowk Area

12 km — Emerging

Pirangut

An emerging township belt beyond Bhugaon. Pirangut is attracting early-stage residential projects with large land parcels. Currently at nascent stage — suited for long-term horizon investors comfortable with longer gestation.

15 km — Leisure

Paud Town

The namesake of Paud Road, Paud is a small market town known for its weekend getaway appeal. Predominantly agricultural and bungalow plots, with some resort-style weekend home developments emerging.

25 km — Nature

Mulshi

The scenic terminus of the Paud Road corridor. Home to Mulshi Lake and lush Western Ghats. Primarily farmhouses, eco-resorts, and weekend retreats. Not a primary residential investment zone but popular for holiday homes.

Infrastructure Developments Driving Paud Road Growth

Planned and completed infrastructure upgrades making Paud Road a top investment corridor

🛣️

Chandani Chowk Flyover

The completed Chandani Chowk grade separator has dramatically reduced travel time from Paud Road to central Pune. Commute to Deccan / Shivajinagar reduced by 15–20 minutes during peak hours.

✓ Completed
🔄

PMRDA Ring Road Interchange

The proposed PMRDA Pune Ring Road passes near Bhugaon, with an interchange planned near the Forest Trails stretch. This will provide direct connectivity to Hinjewadi IT Park and the Pune bypass.

🚧 In Progress
🏗️

Paud Road Widening

The widening of Paud Road to a 4-lane carriageway between Chandani Chowk and Bhugaon is underway, significantly improving traffic flow and reducing commute times along the entire corridor.

🚧 In Progress
🔀

Bavdhan–Pirangut Bypass

A proposed bypass road connecting Bavdhan directly to Pirangut (bypassing Bhugaon town centre) would further reduce intra-corridor travel time and open up new development land parcels.

📋 Proposed
🏫

Social Infrastructure Expansion

Multiple international schools, hospitals, and retail centres have come up in Bavdhan and Bhugaon since 2020, making the Paud Road belt increasingly self-sufficient for daily urban needs.

✓ Operational
🌐

Hinjewadi IT Park Connectivity

Paud Road provides one of the quickest access routes to Hinjewadi IT Park (Phase I–III), home to 300,000+ IT professionals. Bhugaon residents commute to Hinjewadi in 20–30 minutes.

✓ Active

Frequently Asked Questions — Paud Road Area Guide

What localities are on Paud Road Pune?
Paud Road starts at Chandani Chowk junction and passes through Bavdhan (4 km), Bhugaon (8 km), Pirangut (12 km), and reaches Paud town (15 km). Major townships along Paud Road include Paranjape Forest Trails (Bhugaon), various Bavdhan projects, and emerging Pirangut developments.
Is Paud Road a good investment corridor?
Yes. Paud Road is one of Pune's fastest-appreciating real estate corridors. Properties in Bhugaon and Bavdhan along Paud Road have seen 18–22% CAGR since 2021. Key drivers: Chandani Chowk flyover, PMRDA Ring Road, proximity to Hinjewadi IT Park, and limited supply of quality developments.
What is the property rate on Paud Road in 2026?
Property rates vary along Paud Road: Near Chandani Chowk area: ₹15,000–20,000/sqft. Bavdhan (4 km from Chandani Chowk): ₹12,000–16,000/sqft. Bhugaon (8 km): ₹8,000–12,000/sqft for apartments, ₹6,500–10,000 for NA plots. Bhugaon offers the best value-to-appreciation ratio on the entire Paud Road stretch.
Why is Paranjape Forest Trails significant for Paud Road real estate?
Forest Trails is the anchor development on the Paud Road–Bhugaon stretch. A 190-acre township of this scale creates permanent social infrastructure (school, clubhouse, roads, security) that raises the entire micro-market. Properties within 2–3 km of Forest Trails have appreciated significantly since the township's development began.
