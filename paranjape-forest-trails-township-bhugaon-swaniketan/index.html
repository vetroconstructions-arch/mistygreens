A Home for the Differently-Abled & Their Families

Differently-abled individuals and the parents who care for them face many unique challenges in daily life. Swaniketan was conceptualized by Paranjape Special Projects Division (PSPD) to create a supportive community where parents and their differently-abled loved ones reclaim freedom and live with dignity.

Situated inside the 190+ acre Forest Trails Township at Bhugaon, Pune, Swaniketan combines residential family units, hostel facilities for independent living, caregiver spaces on every floor, and specialized therapy centers.

Integrated Care & Vocational Infrastructure

🛠️ Vocational Centre

Helps differently-abled individuals gain a foothold in society through meaningful work, skill development, and purpose in life.

🧸 Day Care Centre

Lets parents reclaim normalcy in their lives while ensuring their child is in safe, professional, and nurturing hands.

🩺 Therapy Spaces

Dedicated clinical therapy center assisting with developmental delays, sensory integration, physical therapy, and cognitive support.

6-Step Assessment & Safeguarding Process

  1. Fill out health & psychosocial assessment form for the Persons with Special Needs (PwSN).
  2. Meeting with Tarangan psychosocial assessment panel.
  3. Comprehensive family + PwSN interaction session.
  4. Financial assessment to ensure long-term care funding security.
  5. Establishment of legal Will & Private Trust for the PwSN.
  6. Final clearance before unit booking & possession from PSCL.
