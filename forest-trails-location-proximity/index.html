Location & Proximity.

The center of West Pune's future. Perfectly positioned between the tranquility of Mulshi and the urban hubs of Kothrud and Bavdhan.

The Gateway to West Pune.

Paranjape Forest Trails in Bhugaon offers a location advantage that is unparalleled in Pune West. With the new flyover at Chandani Chowk and the upcoming Ring Road, connectivity to Hinjewadi IT Park, Baner, and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway is efficient and sustainable. Enjoy a forest life while staying connected to the city's finest schools and hospitals.

  • Hinjewadi: Optimized commute via the bypass.
