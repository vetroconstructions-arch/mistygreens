FAQs

What is stamp duty on NA plots in Pune 2026?

5% for male buyers, 4% for female buyers in PMRDA area (Bhugaon, Bavdhan, Paud Road). Plus 1% registration charges. A ₹1.23Cr plot = ₹7.38L total for male buyer.

Can I pay stamp duty online in Maharashtra?

Yes — pay via igr.maharashtra.gov.in using GRAS system. Accept net banking, credit card, UPI. Get e-challan instantly. Required before Sub-Registrar appointment.

Is stamp duty included in home loan?

No — stamp duty and registration are NOT covered by home loans or plot loans. These must be paid separately from your own funds at the time of registration. Plan ₹6–8L extra for a ₹1.23Cr plot.

What documents are needed at Sub-Registrar for plot registration?

Aadhaar card, PAN card, Sale Agreement, e-stamp duty challan, 2 passport photos, property title documents, NOC from developer. The developer's representative will accompany you.
