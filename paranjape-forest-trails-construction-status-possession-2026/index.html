Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the construction status of Everglades at Paranjape Forest Trails?

Everglades (1 & 2 BHK PRO Homes from ₹48.50 L*, MahaRERA: P52100055711) is under active rapid construction with structural RCC frameworks advancing on schedule in alignment with MahaRERA milestone commitments.

Which enclaves at Paranjape Forest Trails are ready to move?

Ready-to-move and delivered enclaves include Misty Greens Phase 1 (NA villa plots ready for home construction from ₹1.23 Cr*), The Cliff Lifestyle Club (fully operational), Sri Sri Ravishankar Vidya Mandir (fully operational school), and earlier phases of The Rivolo and The Canopy apartments.

What construction technology is used at Forest Trails Bhugaon?

Paranjape Schemes employs precision aluminum formwork (Mivan technology), seismic-zone-compliant reinforced concrete, high-efficiency storm-water drainage networks, and factory-finished joinery ensuring long-term structural durability.

How does Paranjape ensure timely possession under MahaRERA?

Under MahaRERA mandates, 70% of buyer collections are held in dedicated project escrow accounts strictly disbursed only against certified engineer and architect completion milestones, preventing fund diversion.

Can I visit the construction site to inspect ongoing progress?

Yes, guided on-site construction walkthroughs are available 7 days a week from 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM with safety equipment and developer relationship managers at the Forest Trails Experience Centre.
