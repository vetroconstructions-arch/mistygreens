1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how paranjapetownship.com ("the Website", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information that you provide when using our website. The Website is operated by PropSmart Realty, the authorised marketing partner for Paranjape Schemes (Construction) Ltd. for the Paranjape Forest Trails township project located at Bhugaon, Paud Road, Pune West.

By accessing or using this website, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the website immediately.

This policy is governed by the Information Technology (Amendment) Act, 2008 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, applicable in India.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you interact with our website — particularly when submitting property enquiry forms or contact forms — you may provide the following personal data:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (for call / WhatsApp follow-up)
  • Budget range and property preferences (plot, apartment, villa)
  • Preferred enclave or unit type
  • Any messages, questions, or special requirements submitted via forms

Submission of this information is entirely voluntary. However, without it, we may not be able to respond to your enquiry effectively.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical data is collected automatically by our analytics tools:

  • Browser type, version, and language preference
  • IP address (anonymized by Google Analytics)
  • Pages visited, time spent, and navigation path on the website
  • Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop) and operating system
  • Referring website or search term that brought you to us
  • Geographic region at city/country level (not precise GPS location)
  • Cookies set by Google Analytics and essential website functionality

This data is collected in aggregate and is used solely to improve the website experience. We do not link anonymized analytics data to individual identities.

3. How We Use Your Information

The personal data you share with us is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries: To contact you via phone, WhatsApp, or email to address your property enquiry.
  • Scheduling site visits: To arrange guided visits to Paranjape Forest Trails, Bhugaon.
  • Sharing property information: To send you brochures, floor plans, price lists, and RERA-registered documents relevant to your enquiry.
  • Follow-up communication: To follow up on your interest via calls or messages, within a reasonable period.
  • Website analytics: To understand aggregate usage patterns and improve content, navigation, and performance.
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable Indian laws and RERA regulations if required.
We will never: Sell, rent, or share your personal information with unrelated third parties, real-estate aggregators, spam marketers, or data brokers.

We use a small number of third-party service providers who may process data on our behalf. Each is bound by their own privacy policies:

4.1 Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our website. GA4 collects anonymized usage data including page views, session duration, device type, and approximate geography. IP addresses are anonymized by default. This data is processed by Google Ireland Ltd. and stored on Google servers.

You can opt out of GA4 tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4.2 FormSubmit

Property enquiry and contact forms on this website use FormSubmit.co to route form submissions to our designated email address. FormSubmit processes submitted form data (name, phone, email, message) to facilitate this delivery. Submissions are not stored by FormSubmit beyond the delivery period.

FormSubmit's Privacy Policy: https://formsubmit.co/privacy

4.3 Cloudflare

This website uses Cloudflare for performance optimization, content delivery (CDN), and security services including DDoS protection. Cloudflare may process visitor IP addresses and set security cookies. Data is processed in accordance with Cloudflare's standard data processing agreement.

Cloudflare's Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

Note on Embedded Content Pages on this website may contain embedded maps (Google Maps) or embedded content from other websites. These embedded services have their own privacy policies and may collect data independently when you interact with them.

5. Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. We use the following types of cookies:

Cookie Type Name / Provider Purpose Duration
Essential Session cookies Required for basic website functionality and session management Session
Analytics _ga, _ga_xxx (Google Analytics) Tracks page views and user sessions for aggregate analytics 2 years
Security __cf_bm (Cloudflare) Bot management and security — distinguishes legitimate users from bots 30 minutes

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to use certain features of this website. For instructions on managing cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org.

6. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative steps to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse:

  • Form submissions are delivered securely to our encrypted Google Workspace (Gmail) account via FormSubmit over HTTPS.
  • Website transmission is secured using SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS). Look for the padlock icon in your browser address bar.
  • No financial data is collected or stored. We do not handle credit card details, bank information, or payment transactions.
  • Data retention: Enquiry data (name, phone, email) is retained for up to 24 months from the date of submission for follow-up and business purposes. After this period, data is deleted from our systems.
  • Access control: Only authorised team members at PropSmart Realty have access to enquiry data. Access is restricted by password-protected accounts.
Important Notice While we implement commercially reasonable security measures, no method of data transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data but are committed to implementing best-practice safeguards.

📋 Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

✏️ Right to Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information we hold about you.

🗑️ Right to Deletion

Request deletion of your personal data from our records ('right to be forgotten').

🚫 Right to Opt-Out

Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time, with immediate effect.

📦 Right to Portability

⚠️ Right to Withdraw Consent

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at: propsmartrealty@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request — [Your Name]". We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

8. Children's Privacy

This website and its services are intended for adults (18 years and above) who are prospective property buyers or investors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us immediately at propsmartrealty@gmail.com and we will promptly delete such information.

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When changes are made:
